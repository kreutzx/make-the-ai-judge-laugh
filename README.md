Entry for [Make the AI Judge Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-rama-melon-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-302d0825) on make.fun.

`main.py` writes a single office-life joke to `/output/entry.txt` — a
first-person confession in one line (119 characters, inside the 200-character
gate): the narrator hides in the fourth-floor stairwell for 47 minutes to
dodge the 3 p.m. standup, and HR books the stairwell as his new office.
One character, one scene, the turn in the final clause.

Run: `python3 main.py` — writes `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 119 characters),
then reads it back and asserts byte equality.
